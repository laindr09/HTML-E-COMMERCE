ABOUT US

Aster began the way most honest things do — with a problem no one else seemed to be solving properly. Years of struggling with acne and sensitive skin meant years of trial and error: harsh actives that promised results and delivered irritation, "gentle" formulas that did nothing at all, and a skincare industry that seemed to force a choice between efficacy and comfort. It felt like no one had built something that respected both the skin and the person in it. So we built it ourselves.

Aster is what we wished existed — skincare formulated with the same care you'd want from a dermatologist, and the same restraint you'd want from someone who actually understands what compromised skin needs. Every formula is built around a simple standard: real, visible results, without the redness, the stinging, or the guesswork.

This isn't a brand built by an outsider looking in. It's built by someone who has stood in front of the mirror, frustrated, and decided to make something better — not just for ourselves, but for everyone who has ever felt like their skin was working against them.
